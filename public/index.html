HOW TO PLAY

01

PICK A CELL

The board is nine mini tic-tac-toe grids arranged in a 3×3. X goes first — click any free cell on the active grid.

02

SEND YOUR OPPONENT

The cell you pick determines which mini-grid your opponent must play on next. Choose carefully.

03

WIN MINI-GRIDS

Win a mini-grid by getting three in a row as usual. Claimed grids are locked — your opponent plays free choice.

04

WIN THE MEGA GRID

Get three of your mini-grid wins in a row across the big board to win the game. Nine arenas, one champion.
